# PaceUp changelog

What changed in PaceUp, newest first, one line per change. Dates are India time.
A line is added here automatically each time a change is merged.

## 26 Nov 2026
- Create activity: cycling activities get a bike type and ride style, a safety note at the end of every cycling WhatsApp post, ⓘ hints on every field, and the Spots box is gone. (PR 61)

## 25 Nov 2026
- New club owners and admins get a "Getting started" guide for their first activity, one reminder after 3 days, and the PaceUp Admin sees who is stuck. (PR 60)
- The sign-in code box no longer gets stuck: it takes digits from any keyboard, has a Paste button and continues by itself. (PR 59)
- Someone signed in without a club now sees a "Bring your club to PaceUp" button. (PR 58)

## 24 Nov 2026
- Sign-in codes still arrive when no club number can send them, from the PaceUp number, with daily limits. (PR 57)
- People can join or drop out until an hour after the start time. (PR 56)

## 23 Nov 2026
- The WhatsApp list is posted again when someone's name is changed. (PR 55)
- WhatsApp lists leave out titles like Dr and Mr in short names. (PR 54)

## 22 Nov 2026
- Update notes go out only from the PaceUp Admin's own WhatsApp, never a club's number. (PR 53)
- The code to link WhatsApp now lasts the full 5 minutes. (PR 52)
- Behind the scenes: every phone alert is logged so missed alerts can be traced. (PR 51)
- A member with a second phone number can be linked to their name by the Lead. (PR 49)
- Members-only activities: members join with their WhatsApp number, others can ask to join. (PR 48)

## 21 Nov 2026
- Linking WhatsApp: clearer "Nothing is sent to you" note and a "That didn't link" screen to try again. (PR 47)
- Phone alerts are offered right after installing the app, with one "alerts are on" message to confirm. (PR 46)
- PaceUp can be installed as an app, and club admins get phone alerts. (PR 45)
- PaceUp Admin: tap Clubs, Users or Memberships to see the full list. (PR 44)
- More help for new clubs at the WhatsApp linking step. (PR 43)
- New club sign-ups are kept for 14 days instead of 24 hours. (PR 42)
- Dashboard: Create activity and Most regular moved to the top. (PR 41)
- The "who didn't turn up" reminder reaches the Lead 1 hour after the start (was 3 hours). (PR 40)
- Club sign-up search shows every club already on PaceUp. (PR 39)
